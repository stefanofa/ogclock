How it works

Chat apps fetch a preview image once, when the link is first shared. ogclock draws that image for the minute it's asked.

  1. 14:32:05

    You copy a link.

    Each copy is a new link. It carries your timezone, nothing else.

  2. 14:32:41

    You paste it in a chat.

    The app asks ogclock for a preview. ogclock draws one for 14:32 and hands it over.

  3. Next Tuesday

    They still see 14:32.

    Apps keep the first preview they fetch, so the minute stays put. That's also why every send gets a fresh link.

Privacy

No tracking, no accounts. The link holds your timezone and nothing else.

Where it works
